# Terms of Service — Get It Done

Provide accurate account and task information. Do not impersonate others, publish unlawful content or access private information without authorization. Task participants agree on scope, timing, safety and qualifications. Identity approval does not prove a background check or professional license.

## Payment terms

The jobber pays the agreed task price plus $1. The tasker pays $1 followed by a percentage on the remaining amount: 2% below $50, 4% from $50 through $99.99, and 5% at $100 or more. The original agreed price sets the tier. Agreements snapshot the breakdown and fee version; percentage deductions round to the nearest USD cent. Prices and offers must be at least $2.

Funding precedes publication. The jobber chooses the tasker. Price adjustments are settled before hiring without applying the connection fee twice. The tasker submits delivery and the jobber approves release. Disputed funds remain frozen for platform review. Pre-hiring cancellation returns all held funds and waives fees. After hiring, request platform review.

An authorized administrator may release, refund or split the payment after reviewing both parties’ evidence, recording a written decision. Full refunds waive all fees. Partial settlements return unused task funds and cap tasker charges to prevent negative payouts.

The currently implemented provider simulates payment operations; checkout states that no actual charge occurs. No actual funds are held or transferred and no bank deposits or withdrawals are provided. Real processing requires a verified provider integration before customer funds are collected.
